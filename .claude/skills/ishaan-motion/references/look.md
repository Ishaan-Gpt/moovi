# Look

## Colour
- Brand palette only, taken from the site's CSS and the logo. Keep tokens in one place (bg, fg, accent, glow + extras).
- **Colour events**: switch light/dark every 1–3 shots so cuts read at speed (cream → ink drop → cream → ink wall → deep-green finale).
- Dark shots: exactly one big soft bloom (radius ≈ 0.6 × frame height, off-centre, slowly rotating/breathing) and an
  optional 5–15% dot grid or dust layer. Light shots: white or pastel with at most one coloured light streak.

## Type
- The site's own fonts. Typical pairing: a geometric/grotesk sans (Plus Jakarta Sans) and a serif italic (Instrument Serif)
  for the hero word. For showreel bookends: a condensed heavy grotesk in a metal gradient + a connected script.
- Measured layout: balanced line breaks, shrink before wrap, clamp into safe areas. Text never overflows.
- Size from the short edge (1080) so 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9 all scale.

## Backdrops (behind every staged shot, on a parallax plane)
grid · dots · rings · giant type (the headline's accent word, huge and faint) · colour block · split · stripes · framed panel.

## Quality laws
- No AI slop, no overlapping elements, phone-safe margins (keep platform UI zones clear on 9:16).
- Real footage beats generated imagery. Brand logos of other companies are only ever stylised stand-ins.
- **Banned unless asked:** crossfades, lens flares, glow on everything, camera shake, random particles.

## Learnt from the research-story-reel reference (2026-10-07)
- **Two worlds**: light (near-white, soft periwinkle bloom rising from the bottom) and dark (near-black, navy bottom glow,
  faint rounded-square tile grid). One accent colour (electric indigo) in both.
- **Hierarchy by weight and colour, not size**: bold = hero noun, accent = payoff word, mid-grey = word still arriving.
  Size changes only once, for the big line.
- **Product UI as stylised white cards**: list with bars, chat bubble, chart, profile message, handwritten sticky notes:
  real-looking content, simplified, 3D-tilted at varied depths with soft shadows and depth of field.
- **Icons**: thin-stroke line icons on blurred, glowing frosted squares; outlined pill chips with a gradient-filled active state;
  connecting lines to imply a workflow.
- Glow means big soft blooms and blurred glass behind things, never a stroke glow on text.
