# Shots and transitions

## Shot design
- One idea, one sentence and one hero word per shot. One signature move per shot.
- Every shot moves the whole time. 3D-UI shots start tilted (~25–35° Y, 10° X) and dolly to frontal.
- Product proof uses the REAL product: screen recordings, real site screenshots, real clips playing inside cards.
  A product card rebuilt from the site's real labels beats any mockup.
- Signature moves that land:
  - logo bars that move like a voice and morph into caption chips on a timeline,
  - a word slam per beat,
  - a lockup that ends at the exact size and place of the site's nav logo (which sets up the match cut),
  - a log-space pull out into a browser,
  - a cursor clicking the real CTA,
  - a coverflow advancing on beats,
  - an odometer counting 01→04 steps,
  - a tilted 3D wall of tiles scrolling in alternating columns, then a dive into one tile,
  - a one-fact-per-beat vertical roll,
  - a keyword orbit around a hero object,
  - a typed pill + cursor click,
  - a pill morphing into an icon,
  - a pixel-built title.

## Transition set (pick by motivation)
| Transition | Use when |
|---|---|
| Morph | Shape A becomes shape B (bars → chips, pill → icon, map → globe) |
| Match cut | Last frame of A equals first frame of B (logo → the same logo in the site nav) |
| Iris / shape wipe | A colour or giant object swallows the frame (cream iris, phone body sweeping) |
| Whip pan | Moving between sections of the same world; add directional motion blur |
| Dive into tile | From an overview into one item, whose colour opens to fill the frame |
| Pixel-block dissolve | Brand-coloured squares 24–48 px, random order, 6–8 frames |
| White flash on motion | 1–2 frames near-white with the incoming shot already moving |
| Rack focus / mosaic→sharp | Revealing a photo or a hero image |
| Negative / x-ray flip | 2–3 frames, before a collage |
| Bar wipe, recede, torn paper | Editorial or kinetic-type pieces |

## Rules
- **No crossfades.** No transition used 3 times in a row. Vary openers across posts.
- Exits are cuts or on-motion wipes, rarely fades. Only the final frame of a piece may fade to black.

## Learnt from the R1 talking-head reference (2026-10-16)
| Transition | How |
|---|---|
| **Slot roll** | Title A becomes title B in place: changed glyphs leave up through a clipped band, new ones rise in, staggered; shared letters stay ("66 SECONDS" → "5 SECRETS") |
| **Dots sucked into the cut** | Count dots ease-`in` to the centre over the last ~0.3 s, then a hard cut to the poster |
| **Pull-out from a close-up of the title** | Cut in at 2.6×, log pull to 1× over ~1.1 s, focus lerping from the word to the frame centre |
| **Dive into its own object** | Chapter card n = log zoom into object n of the hero poster, which lights while the rest defocuses |
| **Rack focus** | The same poster goes sharp → blurred over 0.5 s while one object comes up sharp and lit |
| **Panel wipe / pixel / bars / bridge** | See `talking-head.md`; always draw the outgoing shot under the incoming panel |
| **Strike-through step back** | "Instead of X, say Y": X is struck on "say" and dims to 55% while Y types in |

## Learnt from the research-story-reel reference (2026-10-07)
| Transition | How |
|---|---|
| **Iris born inside the type** | A small dot appears between two words at x-height and balloons (log scale, ~0.13 s) to fill the frame; the dot is the next scene's background colour |
| **Melt to blobs** | Objects defocus and recolour to the accent until they are soft blobs: they "lose their form" (pairs with words like fade/lost/forgotten) |
| **Text blur-out** | The words themselves blur and fade away, leaving an empty frame (only when the words say so) |
| **Push-in cut on motion** | An accelerating push (~1.5×) on a line of type, hard cut mid-move into a new world |
| **Pull-out reveal** | Close on a few elements → camera pulls out to show hundreds → lines connect a few of them into a network |
| **Leftover-element bridge** | Everything fades except one element (a connecting line), which becomes the next scene's first shape (an arrow) |
| **Tracking camera on type** | Huge line typed from the left; the camera tracks right with it and earlier words slide off through an edge fade |
