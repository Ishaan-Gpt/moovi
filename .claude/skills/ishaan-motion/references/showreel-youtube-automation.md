# Sub-skill of Showreel: YouTube / social automation (10-minute story channels)

Process, step by step. Brainstorm WITH Ishaan at each gate; no step sets a taste default; log the time of every step.
Working files: `docs/agents/story/` in the Moovi repo (README, PLAN, RESEARCH, OPEN-DECISIONS).

0. **Honesty rule.** Every time claim is measured and logged beside its estimate. If a target is not possible, say so first.
1. **Think.** Niche, audience, the channel's promise, why a stranger subscribes. Output: a channel thesis (3 lines).
2. **Research.** Channels that grew fast (RESEARCH.md): evidence, not hunches; survivorship bias named. The AI-use
   disclosure of each marked confirmed / likely / unknown.
3. **Why cards.** For the best 3-5 references: shots not frames, one batched call, rules "when X, do Y because Z", the
   variation test (`why-not-what.md`). Store them as Principles.
4. **Style.** 2-3 styles as sets of principles plus improvised elements (never a template). Options go to Ishaan first.
5. **Story to beat sheet.** Premise, hook (first 30 s), the retention job of each beat (open a loop, reveal, reset,
   close), 40-60 beats for 10 minutes, one idea per beat.
6. **Make.** Voice, footage, scenes in parallel, stitch; log seconds per film second per stage.
7. **Check.** Facts and titles checked by a human; one blind-judge call on a contact sheet; originality (not a repeat
   of the last film).
8. **Publish and learn.** Title and thumbnail tests; read the retention graph back into new Principles.

Stop and ask Ishaan at steps 1 and 4, and before any publishing step.

## Rules from Ishaan's corrections (newest on top)
- **2026-10-20, decisions after the measured taste spec (supersede the 3/10-round rules where they differ):** show the REAL thing: real pages,
  apps and the subject's own mark (replaces "a logo only if the subject has one / an API-MCP film has none": MCP has a mark); literal proof
  first, at most one metaphor and never two figurative beats in a row (replaces "one metaphor object per idea"); pacing like the references
  (about 3 words a second, pauses 0.1-0.6 s, the phrase shown slightly ahead of the voice, never a frozen hold); sound is chosen by an A/B of
  two live previews on the same picture (his pack rules vs the references' 2-4 types, about 2 arrivals per 10 s, effects 13-15 dB under the voice).
- **2026-10-20 ("my references are 9/10 and you are still 1/10; you did not extract their taste"): taste is MEASURED, not summarised.**
  Reading 200 px contact sheets and a handful of frames gives platitudes ("one idea per beat"), not taste. Extract per dimension
  (typography, motion, assets/composition, colour/light, editorial/pacing, sound, holistic), each from frames at 4 fps plus 30 fps bursts
  and the audio, with numbers (px, frames, hex, dB) and evidence moments; have every claim re-checked against the frames by a skeptic;
  then compare the film frame by frame against the verified rules before rebuilding anything.
- **2026-10-20 (verdict 1/10 on the hand-built API vs MCP reel): remove the hard-coded WHAT, never the engine's craft.** When a correction says "stop
  using the templates' rules", the fix is to keep what the Style 1 engine does well (its motion feel, camera, floating cards and phones, easing and blur,
  typography, sound mix) and replace only the fixed rules and invented props. Never rebuild the film as flat shapes in a plain page. Before a rebuild,
  ask which parts of the old output Ishaan liked, keep those, and show him a short frame check against the old film.
- **2026-10-20 (verdict 3/10 on the second API vs MCP reel), the look is derived, never inherited.** (1) Do not reuse library or old-template assets by default:
  read the WHY of the reference's background, pictures, UI components, effects and text, then create assets for THIS subject (any tool is allowed;
  the output decides). (2) Text is one ink colour plus grey; emphasis is weight and size only, never a hard-coded multicolour or serif-gold accent;
  colour appears only where the subject has one. (3) A logo appears only if the subject has one (an API/MCP film has none; never the stage site's
  logo in the corners). (4) Animation is chosen by what the sentence MEANS (assemble, draw, morph, travel, count, highlight), never one slide-in for
  the whole film. (5) Speed comes from the slowest thing: the minimum time a text or a component needs to be read or understood decides how fast
  the other animations run; nothing leaves before it was readable; if the voice is faster, merge beats, never rush the object. (6) The explanation
  must TEACH: a viewer who already knows the topic still understands every beat; one concrete metaphor object plus one mechanism animation per
  idea; the viewer stays engaged through the whole film. (7) Use ALL the premium sound set (not a few repeated sounds), placed by event.
  (8) The channel's tagline is "Your daily dose of AI".
- **2026-10-20, a film's text is the approved script, all of it.** Style 1's first topic reel scored 2/10: about 20% of the approved
  script never reached the screen and the layout rules were hard-coded "what" with no "why". The approved words must all appear
  (as captions or titles); a QA or layout step may re-time or restyle text but never drop it. Replace hard-coded layout rules
  with principles (why-not-what.md) before building more on Style 1.
- **2026-10-20, brands are never forced.** A channel reel about a topic (e.g. "API vs MCP: what's the difference?") does
  not get a "Meet <Brand>" line or a domain line added. A script doctor must not rewrite a good script to satisfy a
  brand template: brand lines are used only when the brief IS a brand. (Engine follow-up: Style 1's script doctor and
  showreel.md "What a Showreel is" still enforce brand lines; change them only with Ishaan's go.)
- **2026-10-20, a script must hook.** "Explains the idea" is not enough: open with the viewer's own situation or the
  video's own title question, give one concrete example that carries the whole difference, end on one quotable line.
  Offer 2-3 different-angle drafts and let Ishaan pick (A calendar example, B jargon-fatigue, C one request step by step).
- **2026-10-20, approve the script before any run.** Never start a render or a Style run on an unapproved script.
- **2026-10-20, voice is taste.** List candidate voices with playable samples of the real first lines; Ishaan picks. Never keep the engine's default voice because it was there.
