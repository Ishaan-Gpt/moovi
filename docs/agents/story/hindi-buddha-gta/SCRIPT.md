## Hindi: Roman (for captions)
0. GTA 6 mein Buddha, duniya ka sabse hinsak game, aur ek aisa aadmi jisne kasam khaayi hai ki kisi ko zara si bhi chot nahi pahunchayega.
1. Game unhe neon roshni se bhari ek sadak par utaarta hai, aur screen par pehla mission chamakta hai, ye car chura lo.
2. Toh Buddha car ke maalik ke paas jaate hain, shaanti se uski aankhon mein dekhte hain, aur chaabi wapas uske kaanpte haath mein rakh dete hain.
3. Laal wanted star ki jagah screen par ek sunehra star jal uthta hai, aur hairaan maalik chupchaap unke peeche peeche chalne lagta hai.
4. Tabhi ek gang chaaron taraf se gher leti hai, sabki bandookein tani hain, aur game ka bas ek hi hukum hai, hathiyaar uthao.
5. Ab faisla aapka hai, jaan bacha ke bhaago ya game ki tarah lado, par Buddha in dono mein se kuch nahi karte.
6. Woh sadak ke beechon beech paalthi maar ke baith jaate hain, aankhein band kar lete hain, aur chaaron taraf ke shor mein bhi unki saans nahi kaanpti.
7. Gang ka leader seedha unke paas aakar bandook unke chehre par taan deta hai, aur is game mein pehli baar koi trigger nahi dabata.
8. Ek ek karke saari bandookein neeche jhuk jaati hain, jab tak poora shehar ruk kar sadak par baithe us aadmi ko dekhne nahi lagta.
9. Agar aap GTA 6 mein hote, toh kya Buddha ki tarah khel paate? Comment mein likho.

## English (for the voice and captions)
0. Buddha in GTA 6, the most violent game ever made, played by a man who has sworn he will never hurt a single living thing.
1. The game drops him onto a street soaked in neon, and the very first mission flashes up on the screen: steal this car.
2. So he walks up to the owner, looks him calmly in the eye, and places the car keys back into the man's shaking hand.
3. Instead of a red wanted star, a golden one lights up on the screen, and the owner, completely lost, starts quietly following him down the street.
4. That's when a gang closes in from every side with their guns raised, and the game flashes its only instruction: pick up a weapon.
5. Now the choice is yours, run for your life or fight the way the game wants you to, but Buddha does neither.
6. He sits down cross-legged in the middle of the road and closes his eyes, and even with the whole street screaming, his breathing never changes.
7. The gang leader walks right up and points his gun at his face, and for the first time in this game, nobody pulls the trigger.
8. One by one the guns come down, until the whole city has stopped just to watch a man sitting quietly in the road.
9. If you were dropped into GTA 6, could you play it the way Buddha did? Tell me in the comments.
