# Long form v6: why it is cut this way

v5 played like a report, and its own demo was weaker than the hits it compared itself with (Ishaan, 2026-11-03). v6 is
built for strangers on YouTube, on the retention curve of this week's breakouts in the niche (Mr omi 334K on 7.7K subs,
Baptiste ODonovan 157K on 3.6K, Michael Szach 71K on 1.5K, Nafay 3D 165K; sheets and transcripts in
`data/refs/explainers/`, local only).

- **Reveal first, with its own sound (0:00).** Stickman Games episode 1 plays before any narration: the count and Giant's fall.
- **The objection killed in the first 20 s.** "No AI video generator touched it, because all of it is code." The hits it beats come straight after (Ifverse 4.6M, Minute Zero 2.2M).
- **A promise and a map by 0:25.** Five numbered steps on screen, with step four tagged as "the part every tutorial skips".
- **The problem is ours.** The first version, shown as itself, scored 2/10 because nobody could follow it.
- **Steps 1-5 are mostly moving picture.** Each step shows the real work (the transcripts, `kit/cast.js`, the judge's prompt and notes) only for as long as the line needs. Every claim is then shown on the episode's own footage. The narrated shots crop the episode's captions off, so the words never fight the voice.
- **A re-hook mid-film.** The save scene plays with its own sound between steps 3 and 4.
- **The failure at about 60%.** The blind judge's 5/10 and its notes play over the old footage. Then the fix, then the climb 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 9.
- **A reason to stay to the end.** The episode's cliffhanger plays before the gift: the one-command install, readable long enough to copy, and the full episode on Ordinary Odd.

Data is honest: clips 1-2 scored 9/10 and clips 3-4 scored 7.0 with blind judges; clip 3's judges went 5 → 6 → 6 → 7 → 7.0.
No tutorial in the niche holds 80-90%; the best hold about 50-60%, and v6 is cut to reach that.
