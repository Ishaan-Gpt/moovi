# Why this video exists (v5, after Ishaan's "very bad" on v4)

## Why Ishaan asked for it
A first long form for his new channel that rides a rising search ("how to make viral animated videos with Claude", 100K-700K views on
small channels this month), proves he can do it, and hands the viewer something real: his free skill.

## Why a viewer clicks
"I want to make one of these myself, tonight, with Claude." They leave happy only if they can do it after watching.

## What they must be able to do after watching (the spine; every section is one of these)
1. **Find what's working:** the prompt that lists the genre's top videos, and the trick of dividing views by subscribers to find breakouts.
2. **Make Claude really watch the hits:** "analyse this video" makes it skim. Tell it to take one frame a second and write out every word.
   The four rules it found go to the viewer as rules they can use.
3. **Pick an idea nobody has made:** the prompt that searches YouTube for copies first; one number over the head; only actions you can animate.
4. **Write the script with the rules:** the prompt, and the opening it produced.
5. **Voice it for free:** Fish Audio's free model, plus word timing so the number changes on the right word.
6. **Build the picture:** the prompt for 2D that feels 3D (why not real 3D: our 3D try scored 5/10), and the result.
7. **Judge it blind and fix it:** the prompt, the real notes, the fixes (4.3 → 5.5, past the 4.6M opening).
8. **Get all of it in one command:** the skill.

## How it must play (what v4 got wrong)
- Every prompt is shown big, in a clean window, long enough to pause and copy (and it's in the repo, PROMPTS.md).
- Every real output is shown readable, never zoomed into blurry screenshot text.
- Never a blank or black player: everything is preloaded behind a loading gate before Play.
- A calm camera: gentle moves and no jumps. A step label keeps the viewer oriented.
- Watched in real time in a browser before it is sent.
