## What the titles share
- **The shape is "How to Make [adjective] [format] With [Claude / Opus 5.5] ([Full Workflow] / 100% FREE)".** "Viral", "Insane",
  "FREE" and "Full Workflow" or "Full Guide" carry most of the top titles. The model name "Opus 5.5" sits in 9 of the top 20.
- **Two hot sub-niches meet here:** Claude animation tutorials (Mr omi, motion graphics), and **free stickman animation**
  (Mark Ai Guy 516K, Jacksons AI 428K and 183K, The AI Blueprint 185K). Nobody in the list makes **3D** stickmen with Claude.
- **Length:** the breakouts run 8-20 min; 11-13 min is the middle.
- **Small channels win on proof plus a free give-away:** Mr omi's free repo, "100% FREE", "free forever".

## What this means for Ishaan's video
The gap is **3D stickman films with Claude, free, full workflow**: the stickman demand plus the Claude-animation demand, with a
look none of them has (a lit 3D world and a 50-character cast). One honest catch on "free": the tools are free (three.js,
ffmpeg, free voices, the skill), but Claude Code needs a Claude plan. The title can say "free" about the workflow and the skill,
and the script says plainly what costs money.
