## Our film inside the film (the "mine" beside theirs)
The first 60 s of a What If episode (the cold open, which is what decides whether a viewer stays), in our 3D stickman look,
with our own premise. It sits beside Ifverse's and Miko's cold opens, so viewers compare like with like.

Premise candidates, gap-checked on YouTube today:
1. **"What If You Only Had 1,000 Words Left?"** (pick): a lifetime word counter over every head. Nobody in the genre has it; the
   only near match is a Roblox story at 89K views, which shows the demand. It uses the genre's proven device (a number over the head),
   and its acting is talking, stopping and looking, which the kit has already proven. It is also a feeling the viewer gets in one second.
2. "What If You Could See How Many Steps Everyone Has Left?": open too, but it needs walking, which the kit hasn't proven.
3. "What If Gravity Switched Off for a Minute Every Hour?": a strong 3D picture, but crowded (2.3M and 305K already made it,
   though not as stickman films), and floating bodies aren't proven on our kit.

Note on the 2026-10-23 rule ("a new superpower in Ifology's 'What IF You Could X' shape is a copy"): a tutorial about this genre
has to show a film in its shape, so the demo uses the title shape on purpose, with a premise nobody has made.
