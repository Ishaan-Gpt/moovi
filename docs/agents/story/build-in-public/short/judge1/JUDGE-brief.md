# Blind judge brief: film X (a 46 s animated stickman short, 16:9)

You have not seen this film before and know nothing about how it was made. Score what is on the frames.

Material: contact sheets of the exported MP4, one frame every 0.5 s with the film time stamped (sheet-*.jpg), plus hook.jpg
(the first 3 s at 4 fps). The narration script is below; the captions on screen follow it.

Narration:
> Fifty people with superpowers just lost a game to the only player in the arena who doesn't have one. The rule is simple: while the
> clock is running nobody moves, and anyone who even twitches drops straight through their gold circle. Turbo lasted less than a
> second, because his whole power is that he can never stop running, so the floor opened under his running feet before anyone else
> had even blinked. Reflex was faster than anyone alive, which turned out to be his problem, because when a fly landed on his nose,
> his hand swatted it before his brain could say no. One by one the circles opened, until only one player was left standing, number
> fifty, with no power at all. Unless you count being very, very good at doing nothing, which, it turns out, is the rarest power in
> the whole game.

Score 1-10 each, with one line of evidence (film time) per score:
1. Hook: is the story and the stakes clear by second 3?
2. Story readability: does every beat (the rule, the proof, Turbo, Reflex and the fly, the wave, #50) read from the pictures alone?
3. Composition: framing, clear subject, nothing blocking faces, captions clear of faces.
4. Craft: lighting, grade, depth, render faults (stray lines, clipping, popping, floating), character acting.
5. Logic: do the pictures agree with the narration and with each other (who falls when, the clock)?
6. Premium: would this pass as a channel's real upload next to the best animated stickman videos?

Then: the mean, and a numbered list of the concrete defects, most important first, each with its film time and a fix in one line.
Be strict: 8 means "ready to show the owner", 10 means "nothing to fix". No praise padding.
