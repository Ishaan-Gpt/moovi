# The Stillest Man Alive (stickman short, about 50 s)

Approved direction: Ishaan, 2026-11-03 ("do something and start"). One narrator: Fish Audio, Ethan, s2.1-pro-free.

Fifty people with superpowers just lost a game to the only player in the arena who doesn't have one.
The rule is simple: while the clock is running nobody moves, and anyone who even twitches drops straight through their gold circle.
That was Turbo, who lasted less than a second, because his whole power is that he can never stop running, so the floor opened under his running feet before anyone else had even blinked.
Reflex was faster than anyone alive, which turned out to be his problem, because when a fly landed on his nose, his hand swatted it before his brain could say no.
One by one the circles opened, until only one player was left standing, number fifty, with no power at all.
Unless you count being very, very good at doing nothing, which, it turns out, is the rarest power in the whole game.
