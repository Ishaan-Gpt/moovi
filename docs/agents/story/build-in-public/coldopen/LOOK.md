# LOOK: "1,000 Words Left" cold open (locked 2026-11-03, after three still rounds)

Medium: 2D with depth on one canvas (ishaan-motion 1.17.74), the genre's own medium (STUDY-whatif.md: the top three are 2D that feels 3D).
- **Light:** black silhouettes against bright light. A café 3,400 px wide, with a sunset window (pistachio, lemon, peach, coral), lemon
  lamps and glowing city windows. The camera picks a different part of the room per shot: the shelves, the window with the sun, or the counter.
- **Palette:** matcha dusk. Sage and pistachio walls, lemon light, aubergine tables and shadow, ivory counters. Coral is the one accent
  (under 10%): the toothbrush, the dog's nose, the party hat, and a low counter. Neutrals are tinted green, so no pure black or white.
  This is not Ifverse's violet and amber, nor Ifinite's rain grey.
- **Cast:** a matte ink ball head; the face is ivory eyes and a mouth line only; one colour per person (you: aubergine jacket;
  the friend: pine jacket with a mustard scarf; the boy: sage). Every silhouette has a lemon rim on the side the light comes from.
- **Depth:** a blurred foreground (heads, a shoulder), a sharp subject, a softly blurred room (3-10 px), and slow push-ins.
- **The counter:** an ivory pill with tabular Bricolage Grotesque 800; a ring pulses out and it bounces on every tick; it turns coral when low.
- **Grade:** a light warm overlay, a vignette, fine grain (alpha 13) and a thin letterbox. No captions, as in all seven references.
- **Sound:** a synthesised pad (Dmaj7, Bm9, Gmaj7, Aadd9), a bell on every counter tick, a swell into the title, and café air. Mixed at -14 LUFS, TP -1.2.
