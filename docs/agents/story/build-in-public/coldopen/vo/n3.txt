He cries into his cappuccino, which is fair, because that was one of the last forty words you will ever say out loud. The average person says about sixteen thousand words a day and never thinks about a single one of them. You were given one thousand, for your whole life, on the morning you turned ten. So, what if you only had a thousand words left? The rules arrived that same morning, written in the fog on the bathroom mirror: every word you say out loud comes off the counter, a whisper costs the same as a shout, and writing things down costs nothing at all. Which is why, by lunchtime, your mum had a drawer full of your notes, and you had already spent eleven words on a joke about the dog, which, to be fair, was a very good joke.
