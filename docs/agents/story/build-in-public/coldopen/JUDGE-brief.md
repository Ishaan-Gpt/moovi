# Blind judge brief: three cold opens of animated stickman "What If" videos (16:9)

You know nothing about how any of them were made. Score only what is on the frames and in the transcripts.

Material, per film (X, Y, Z): contact sheets of the opening, one frame every 0.5 s or every 1-2 s as labelled (sheet-*.jpg in the
film's folder), plus its narration transcript with times (transcript.txt).

Score each film 1-10 on each line, with one line of evidence (a film time):
1. **Hook:** by second 5, do I know who this is, what is strange, and why I should keep watching?
2. **Story readability:** does each beat read from the pictures alone (who wants what, what it costs, what changed)?
3. **Composition:** a clear subject, clean framing, nothing clipped or crowded, text never over a face.
4. **Look and craft:** light, depth, colour and finish (render faults, flat areas, muddy grade, weak silhouettes).
5. **Acting:** do the faces and bodies sell the feeling of each beat?
6. **Premium:** would this pass as the opening of a 1M-view video in this genre?

Then for each film: the mean, and a numbered list of its concrete defects, most important first, each with a film time and a one-line fix.
Last: rank X, Y and Z. Be strict: 8 means "ready to show the owner", 10 means "nothing to fix". No praise padding.
