Step two is where most people go wrong. If you just ask Claude to analyse a video, it looks at a handful of frames and guesses, so tell it exactly how to watch: the first ninety seconds of the top seven, one frame every second, and every word of the narration written out with a timestamp. Then ask for a rulebook of what all of them share, measured, not opinions. Mine came back with four rules you can use straight away. First, prove the power before you name it, the way Ifverse shows a fly being caught, then a falling coffee cup, and only shows the title after thirty-five seconds. Second, put one number on screen, normal against you, like a quarter of a second against two and a half hundredths. Third, give a friend the line the viewer is thinking, "how did you do that?" And fourth, slow down to about one and a half words a second, and let the silence do the work.
