Step three is the idea, and this is where copies die. Before you write anything, have Claude search YouTube for every premise on your list. "What if you could rewind ten seconds" already has at least eight copies, and the best of them has thirteen thousand views, so it's gone. "What if you only had a thousand words left for your whole life" had nobody in the genre, and a Roblox story on the same idea had eighty-nine thousand views, so people want it. Keep one number over the head, here a word counter, and choose a story where the characters only need to talk, look and go quiet, because every action you can't animate well is a scene you'll end up cutting.
