Step one is finding what's actually working. I asked Claude for the most viewed What If stickman videos of the last month, with views and subscribers for each, plus one extra column: views divided by subscribers. That column is the trick. A big channel getting views tells you nothing, but Miko getting almost seven hundred thousand views with only seven thousand subscribers tells you the idea itself is hot. Sorted that way, a pattern jumps out, because Ifverse, Minute Zero and Ifinite are all small channels, and every one of them passed a million views.
