The idea is where most copies die, so Claude searched every premise on my list before we wrote a word, and threw most of them out. "What if you could rewind ten seconds" has been made at least eight times this month, and the best of those copies has thirteen thousand views. "What if you only had a thousand words left for your whole life" hadn't been made by anyone in the genre, and the closest thing on YouTube was a Roblox story with eighty-nine thousand views, which told us people want it and nobody had served it yet. It also keeps the genre's best trick, a number over the head, and it asks nothing of the characters except talking, looking and going quiet, which matters more than it sounds, because every action you can't animate well is a scene you'll end up cutting.
