Step six is the picture, and I learned this one the hard way. My first try was proper 3D, and two blind judges gave it five out of ten, because the legs couldn't run and the hand couldn't reach the face. The biggest films in this genre are flat 2D that only feels 3D, so ask for exactly that: black ball heads with a rim of light on one side, a bright room the camera drifts across, a blurred foreground and one light mood. Claude built mine on a single canvas, entirely in code, twenty-one shots in sixty-one seconds.
