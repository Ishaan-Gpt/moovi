Step two is the cast, and this is where code beats every image generator. Each of the fifty players is a few lines of code: a colour, a number and one power with its own look. Turbo can't stop running, Clockwork ages a year every minute, Reflex reacts ten times faster, and Copycat, nobody knows yet. Because they're code, they look the same in every shot, from every angle, in every episode, which is the one thing AI video still can't promise. The arena is code too, fifty gold circles around a billion-dollar trophy, and episode two starts from it instead of from nothing.
