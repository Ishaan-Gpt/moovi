# Moovi demo film: final script (read-aloud)

About 3:00. Hook A and the safe time-stamped line (switch either if you prefer). The on-screen proof per beat is in
SCRIPT.md.

**1 · Hook (0:00–0:12)**
Building a SaaS is dead in 2026… (beat, silence)
…if you can't get it seen.
The product is a commodity now.
Building it is twenty percent. Getting it seen is the other eighty.
And getting seen means content.
Most founders can't make it. Or just don't want to.

**2 · The wave (0:12–0:30)**
We had AI video generators. Now there's a new wave: videos written in code. Every frame is code, every cut is a
decision you can read. After vibe coding comes vibe editing. And we're riding it.

**3 · Meet Moovi (0:30–0:50)**
This is Moovi, your video production team. A director, Moovi, and a crew of seven agents: Editor, Researcher,
Writer, Sound, Designer, Lens and Keeper. Three presets. A Launch video to start your brand. A Showreel to promote
it. And Edit, when you want to show your face, cut like a professional editor would.

**4 · Demo (0:50–1:20)**
Pick a preset. Drop your clip, and choose the part you want edited. Now watch the crew work, each at its own desk.
You get a live preview, not a slow render: captions, sound, motion. Don't like something? Just say it.
"Make the captions bigger."

**5 · The graph is the core (1:20–2:05)**
Moovi's memory is FalkorDB. Not a side database: the core. Four graphs on one instance: the craft library,
accounts, one graph per user, one per project. Every read is one of 248 named Cypher queries, tagged with the agent
that ran it. As a film is made, watch it write: every decision, time-stamped, linked "because" to the rule or the
feedback behind it. So the graph remembers what you said, and when.
Ask "why these captions?", and Moovi answers with a shortest path, from the decision back to what you told it two
videos ago. PageRank finds what a style leans on too much. Shortest paths plan the crew's route.
That's Track 1: agents that act on connected data and explain themselves. And Track 2: memory per user, shared by
the whole crew.

**6 · Pull the plug (2:05–2:20)**
Still think the graph is optional? Let's stop it.
Moovi stops. "Moovi's memory is offline." No silent fallback. Start it again, and Moovi's back.

**7 · The harness (2:20–2:38)**
Under the hood, Moovi is an agent harness: the loop, the tools, the checks and the memory around the model. Bring
one key, and it uses three of that provider's models: a director model plans, a crew model writes, a fast model
takes the small calls. Or connect Claude Code over MCP: about four thousand tokens a video. A launch video previews
in under thirty seconds. Less cost. Less time.

**8 · Taste (2:38–2:54)**
So how are we different? We go deep on the one thing that matters most right now: taste. An ever-growing library
in the graph: captions, effects, music beds, voices, camera moves, clips, about nine hundred and fifty pieces. We
feed it references, and it breaks them into fundamentals. One global skill, a skill per style, both getting sharper
with every reference.

**9 · CTA (2:54–3:00)**
Moovi. Your video production team. Built on FalkorDB.
