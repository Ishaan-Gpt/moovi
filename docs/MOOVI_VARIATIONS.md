## Mix-and-match modifiers (add one at the end of any prompt)

- **Style:** "Pop Mart flocked vinyl figure" · "Jellycat plush photographed in a studio" · "soft claymation look" · "Pixar feature film still"
- **Cuteness:** "even bigger eyes, smaller body" · "chubbier cheeks" · "sleepy half-closed eyes"
- **Fur:** "shorter velvety fur" · "longer fluffier fur" · "blue only at the very tips" · "fully ice-blue paws and tuft"
- **Mood:** "giggling" · "proud and confident" · "curious head tilt" · "waving hello"

## How to test

- Generate each prompt twice.
- Shrink the images to about 64 px and compare. **The one that's still cute and recognisable at that size wins.** That size is your favicon, app icon and loader.
- Also check that the silhouette reads as solid black.
